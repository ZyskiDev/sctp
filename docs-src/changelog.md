# Changelog: V1.6 > V2
Thank you for using the SCTP! We updated to V2 on the 22nd of September - download the new mod files via the website.
## Website
::: details Mapart!
- The website now has the full in-game mapart museum, but digital!
- As mapart seller, you can verify your account to manage your own maps!
:::
::: details Collections!
- You can now mark mapart and rares as having them in your collection!
- You can share your collection with others via a link, and you can see a ton of stats by clicking on your username!
:::
::: details Marketplace additions!
- You can now post jobs on the marketplace!
- You can review listings/sellers now!
:::
::: details Rare-dle!
- You can now play a variation on wordle, but based on our SC rares catalog: rare-dle!
- A new rare is chosen every day!
:::
::: details QOL/fixes
- Hide columns from the search table
- Price filter can now convert to diamond-equivalent
- Typo suggestions where needed
- Mapart of the day on the home page
- Navigation bar redesigned
:::

## Mod
::: details Mapart Scanner
- The mod now has a built in mapart scanning feature that uploads straight to the gallery!
:::
::: details Watchlist QOL
- You can now ignore listings in your watchlist
- General usage of watchlist has been improved.
:::
::: details In-game reporting
- You can now report all listings via in-game menu's!
:::
::: details Small extras
