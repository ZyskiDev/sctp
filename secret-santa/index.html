How it works

You get a random player from the same value class, make them a gift, and on Christmas everyone opens the gift someone made for them. Nobody finds out who made theirs.

    High value48 DB or more
    Your gift is worth at least 48 DB, and so is the one you get.
    Low value16 DB or more
    Your gift is worth at least 16 DB, and so is the one you get.

    Sign up

    FireflySecret Santa is Firefly only. Gifts are made, dropped off and opened on Firefly, so sign up with the account you play on Firefly.
    Sign in first (recommended). You can then change your entry until sign-ups close, and import your SCTP wishlist. Without signing in, your entry can't be changed later.

    Category
    You'll make 2 gifts: one high value gift (48 DB+) for one player and one low value gift (16 DB+) for another. You'll get 2 gifts back too.
    Don't match me with

    Up to 5 players you don't want to make a gift for or get one from. Use their full Minecraft username.

    Wishlist

    What would you love to get? Pick rares, items and mapart from SCTP (with their suggested value), or write your own wishes. Your Secret Santa sees this list and where to buy each item on Firefly.

    Your hobbies, what you build, favourite colours, things you collect… anything that helps your Secret Santa pick something you'll love.
